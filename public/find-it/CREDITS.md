- **Map tiles:** © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, loaded at runtime from `tile.openstreetmap.org` for light interactive use under the OSM tile usage policy. Tiles are never prefetched or bundled. Attribution is always visible on the map.
- **Map library:** [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) 1.9.4, BSD-2-Clause.
- **Points:** U.S. Census Geocoder (public domain) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), looked up once on Nov 7, 2026 and stored as static coordinates.
- **Parcel and hazard facts:** OC Public Works GIS (Parcels, Fire Hazard Severity Zones LRA), SCAG LDX layers (zoning, general plan, existing land use, housing element sites), FEMA NFHL. Queried once on Nov 7, 2026; results stored as text with links. Nothing is fetched at runtime.
- **Records:** CEQAnet (State of California) notices SCH 2024080316, 2026050256, 2026100816.
- **Listing facts:** price, HOA, beds, size, year built, MLS number and URL only, from public IDX pages (personal, non-commercial use; this demo links out). No photos, copied descriptions, or agent names.
- **Timetables:** Metrolink Orange County Line weekday schedule and OCTA Route 473 (effective Aug 9, 2026), read Nov 7, 2026.
- The offline basemap file from Census TIGERweb (P2) was not built.
